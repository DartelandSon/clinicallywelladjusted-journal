Hi there! Welcome to the first-ever post on Clinically Well Adjusted, where I’ll share my journey building WeRestore.Tech from scratch, all while navigating life with schizophrenia and making a sustainable tech business a reality.

If you’re reading this, you’re probably here for one of three reasons:

1. You’re curious about how a disabled entrepreneur is bootstrapping a business.

2. You just want to know if I can fix your phone.

3. You took a wrong turn on the internet but decided to stick around anyway (great choice, by the way).

Let me break it down for you: life isn’t simple when you’re living on disability income, running a business from your kitchen table, and trying to save the planet one gadget at a time. But here’s the thing—it doesn’t have to be simple to be worth it.

Through this blog, I’ll share my repair hacks, business wins (and fails), mental health ups and downs, and the occasional bad pun. My YouTube channel will give you the behind-the-scenes chaos—think tech tutorials, real talk about entrepreneurship, and a front-row seat to my experiments in not losing my mind while fixing yours (your tech, that is).

Why am I doing this? Because being real about the struggle is what makes the story worth telling. Too many people on the schizophrenia spectrum are told to lower their expectations of living a productive life. I want my story to inspire hope—for you or for someone else—to believe in the possibility of a better life. Also, because I’ve Googled ‘how to start a sustainable tech business with no money’ way too many times, and it’s about time someone wrote the real guide.

Stick around for repair tips, sustainability insights, and a few laughs along the way. After all, the world might be a mess, but your phone doesn’t have to be.

Thanks for joining me on this wild ride—here’s to trying, failing, and trying again, with style.

Repair, Resell, Recycle!
